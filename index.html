UX case study: Emergency Guidance Robot

Overview

Project: Emergency Guidance Robot and companion app

Team: Group 3

My role: [your role]

Timeline: [e.g., Fall 2026, 5 weeks]

Tools: [Figma, paper sketching, ...]

Summary: During an emergency, people often get an alert that tells them to shelter in place or evacuate but not where to go or how. We designed a campus guidance robot and a companion phone app to fill that gap. The robot uses a calm voice, blue guidance lights, and a clear on-screen arrow to lead people to the nearest safe exit, and it changes its route when something blocks the way. The app shows a live map with danger zones, alert levels, nearby places, and the next turn. We worked from a hand sketch to a high-fidelity app screen and an 8-step user storyboard following one person, a high school senior visiting campus, from the first alert to reaching safety.

Problem statement

Campus visitors and students who are unfamiliar with a building need a way to get calm, step-by-step guidance during an emergency, because a text alert alone leaves them scared, unsure where to go, and likely to freeze or follow a crowd into danger.

Our persona: Maya Thompson, 17, a high school senior visiting campus. She gets an active-shooter alert on her phone in a crowded hallway and doesn't know the building.
